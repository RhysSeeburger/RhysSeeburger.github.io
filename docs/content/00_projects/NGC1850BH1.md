    ### The Curious Case of NGC 1850 BH1

     In 2021, [Saracino et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.06506) discovered a system with a potential dormant stellar mass black hole in NGC 1850, a cluster in the Large Magellanic Cloud. Subsequent discussion by [El-Badry & Burdge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.07925) and [Saracino et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.07369) has called the initial finding into question, highlighting the need for further observations and analysis.
     My work aims to analyse multi-epoch follow-up spectroscopic data gathered with VLT-FLAMES, and identify the components of the binary, constraining their formation histories and stellar parameters. This work is currently underway, and I will update this page with any new findings.
